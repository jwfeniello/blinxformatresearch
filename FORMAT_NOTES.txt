BLINX PLATINUM ASSET FORMAT RESEARCH (in progress)

All offsets below are byte offsets. Unless stated otherwise, integers are little endian.
Converted output is in converted/. Scripts and JSON reports are in the workspace root.

XDVDFS / XISO
Volume descriptor at 0x10000 begins MICROSOFT*XBOX*MEDIA (20 bytes).
Root directory sector and byte size are uint32 at +20 and +24. Sector size 2048.
Directory records: uint16 left/right tree offsets (units of 4 bytes), uint32
sector, uint32 size, uint8 attributes, uint8 name length, then name bytes.
Attribute 0x10 identifies a directory. extract_assets.py exports files and hashes.
Reference: https://github.com/XboxDev/extract-xiso

MIT texture container
Header: 'MIT\0', uint32 entry count, uint32 payload base, uint32 reserved.
Entry table begins at 16; each record is 48 bytes: 40-byte NUL-terminated ASCII
name, uint32 relative payload offset, uint32 payload size. Observed payload base
equals 16 + 48*count in all 76 files. Entries contain ordinary DDS images.
3,008 entries extracted by extract_mit.py. PNG exports contain the base image;
original DDS files preserve mipmaps and encoded pixel data.

DDS / TGA
Pillow decodes the source image into RGBA PNG. Standalone PNG exports are checked
against the decoded RGBA bytes. This does not establish visual correctness or
preservation of every mip level. DDS originals remain available.

ADX / AIX / SFD
ADX is CRI ADPCM. All 69 standalone tracks have PCM16 WAVs at the
declared source sample count, including 44 with RIFF sampler loops.
The WAVs retain the FFmpeg PCM prefix and remove up to 31 frames
beyond the declared sample count. vgmstream confirmed the frame and
loop values independently. See converted/audio_banks/adx/README.txt.
AIXF files contain segmented/interleaved ADX audio. Each of the three
files has one segment and three stereo layers. The nine lossless ADX
exports were checked against 29,838 source packets and the corresponding
channel pairs in the six-channel WAVs. Native DirectSound submissions
route layer 0 to front left/right, layer 1 to back left/right and
layer 2 to center/LFE. The 5.1 WAVs and movie tracks use this mapping.
See converted/audio_banks/aix/README.txt.
SFD files are MPEG program streams with MPEG video and, where present, ADX audio.
All 32 MPEG-1 video streams and 18 embedded ADX streams have lossless
elementary exports, checked against FFmpeg stream-copy hashes. Timestamp
CSVs and private metadata payloads accompany them.
The decoded SofdecStream and CRITAGS JSON covers authoring filenames,
muxer versions, stream IDs, dimensions, rates and picture counts.
The counts match all 42,171 MPEG pictures across the 32 movies.
See converted/movies/sfd/README.txt.
MKV outputs copy video bitstreams and encode decoded audio as FLAC.
The source and MKV clocks agree within 0.5 ms on 42,163 paired video
timestamps. Eight final source timestamps were unavailable. MKV
rounding introduces 131 extra timestamp ties across 11 films. All
18 embedded audio streams match the decoded source PCM hashes.
The 11 affected movies also have 90 kHz NUT playback exports. Their
picture times increase strictly and differ from the comparable SFD
timestamps by at most one tick. The video bitstreams and decoded
embedded audio match their source hashes.
Native startup traces pair end_a01, end_a02 and pr_a01 with external
stereo ADX or three-layer AIX according to 0x4d27ec. The playback
exports retain both tracks in full at a zero start offset. Native
transition traces verify the 60-update countdown, retention of the
first soundtrack when its video closes, and the volume-gated stop
before opening end_a02. Decoder latency and ending scene duration
remain open.

NATIVE ANIMATION MATRIX CONVENTION
Caller 0x9d14f..0x9d238 applies T Rz Ry Rx S to column vectors.
The Y helper receives a negated angle and negates it again internally.
SSE registers XMM4..7 hold matrix columns. Rotation helpers use a
4096-entry sine table at 0x390b88; startup routine 0x1266c0 initializes
index conversion constants. verify_native_matrices.py runs that startup
and the original helpers: 100 random transforms agree with exporter
conventions within 0.002128 (native trigonometric quantization retained).
convert_motion_matrices.py exports 7084 exact native local matrices across
11 NPZ files, each with frame and node indices. All NPZ arrays round-trip
exactly. This resolves local rotation order, not hierarchy evaluation
flags, final mesh binding, or playback speed.

NATIVE HIERARCHY VALIDATION
verify_native_hierarchy.py executes recursive routine 0x9d090, bypassing
only geometry rendering at 0x9ce20. Captures node matrices at 0x9d240.
For 11 candidate bindings at first/middle/last frames, child-first then
sibling traversal and parent*local matrix composition match native output

Deferred polygon material state: consumer identified and native chain verified
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 256-bucket consumer is 0x98d00. It visits bucket heads from 0x8e42fc
backward to 0x8e3f00 and follows record+0x30 links. Type-1 records begin a
strip, type-2 records supply vertices, and type-3 records end a strip.
At 0x98e2b it copies the end record's +8 low byte to the generated 32-byte
draw descriptor at +0x1a. At 0x99129 that byte is sign-extended and passed
to 0x90bb0, the destination blend-factor switch. The similar 16-bucket
consumer starts at 0x99290. Disassembly: transparent_queue_consumer.txt,
transparent_queue_references.txt, transparent_pass_callers.txt in analysis/.

The marker originates in MATERIAL commands, not triangle header bits:
0x9a074..0x9a085 tests (material_command_word & 0x700) == 0x100. The end
record at 0x9a746..0x9a766 stores marker OR global mode 0x6762c4.
verify_deferred_blend_chain.py executes those original blocks, the record

Portable surface wave kernel:
surface_wave.py implements native interior update 0xebc30. It uses two float32 arrays, keeps destination boundaries unchanged, and swaps pointer roles after updating. Coefficients derive from squared sqrt(parameter)*abs(time step), each capped at 0.49. The interior combines the two axis-neighbor sums and a center coefficient 2-2*(cx+cz), subtracts the prior destination value, then applies damping when absolute amplitude reaches the threshold. This kernel alone does not introduce disturbances into an all-zero state.
verify_surface_wave.py compares 150 native updates across three dimensions and five time-step values, including negative and clamped cases. Both arrays, swaps and boundaries match exactly on these fixtures; report analysis/surface_wave_verification.json. General hardware floating-point equivalence beyond tested cases is not claimed.
converted/surface_records/wave_parameters.json captures actual per-record coefficients/damping/threshold by executing the original parameter setup functions with native constructor spacing. Alternate update mode is labelled unported. Boundary forcing, disturbance events, global time-step scheduling and full animated surface export remain unresolved.
